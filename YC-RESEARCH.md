# Climatico YC Research Memo

Written: 2026-11-14. Purpose: evidence base before applying to YC. Verify sources before quoting numbers.

## 1. Market

- Carbon accounting software: $2.05B (2025) → $2.49B (2026) → $5.0B (2030) forecast (PMR-family research).
- The top 3 are all enterprise quote-only: Watershed (tech/enterprise, est. $50–250k+/yr),
  Persefoni (finance/investor-grade disclosure, has a free tier), Sweep (value chain/group
  structures, strong in EU).
- The startup/SMB segment is effectively empty. Matches the PRD's "wrong customer" claim.
- Direct comp is YC alum Bend (S22, carbon accounting API for startups).
  "How are you different from Bend?" is guaranteed.
- Bend outcome (checked Nov 2026): started as API → pivoted in 2023 to a climate-friendly
  corporate card ($2.5M seed) → now renamed "Versa" on YC pages, 2-founder team, Active.
  No breakout, no shutdown on record. Lesson: a spend-based estimates API alone did not work.
  Climatico must answer with real ops data + evidence trail + supplier compliance flow.

## 2. Why now: suppliers are being asked for data right now

- California SB 253: first Scope 1+2 reports due November 10, 2026 (this week). Scope 3 from
  2027, based on 2026 calendar-year data — so large buyers are asking suppliers for 2026
  figures now.
- EU CSRD covers tens of thousands of companies; requests cascade down the value chain.
- Implication: startups do not buy this voluntarily; they buy because a customer (large buyer)
  demands it. The Orepath story (cell buyer asked, deal at risk) is exactly this pattern.
- Caution: coverage mentions litigation history around SB 253. Pitch "regulation + buyer
  demand," never regulation alone.

## 3. ICP hypothesis (to validate)

#1: Seed–Series B suppliers to large buyers. Battery/materials, hardware, manufacturing,
logistics. Trigger is the buyer's emissions data request; buyer is the founder/COO.
Wedge: a price point Watershed cannot reach ($0–5k/yr start) via agent automation.

Validation questions (interviews):
- Received an emissions/ESG data request from a customer in the last 6 months?
- How long / who / how did you answer it?
- Ever lost or stalled a deal for lack of an answer? Deal size?
